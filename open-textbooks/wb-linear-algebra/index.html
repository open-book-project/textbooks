    About this book¶

    A complete undergraduate course in linear algebra, transcribed on Wikibooks from Jim Hefferon's freely licensed textbook: linear systems, vector spaces, maps between spaces, determinants and similarity, each chapter with applied topics, and appendices on proof techniques. The sections are numbered by chapter (I.1, I.2, ...).
