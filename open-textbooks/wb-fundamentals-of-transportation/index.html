    About this book¶

    An introductory course in transportation engineering: networks and travel demand (trip generation, destination, mode and route choice, and evaluation), public transit (demand, capacity, network design and timetabling), planning and operations, queueing, traffic flow, shockwaves and signals, and the geometric design of roads (sight distance, grades, earthwork, horizontal and vertical curves), each topic with a worked problem and its solution.
