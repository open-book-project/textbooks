    About this book¶

    A complete engineering course in control theory: system modelling, Laplace and Z transforms, transfer functions and sampled-data systems, state-space methods, stability (Routh-Hurwitz, Lyapunov), root locus, frequency response with Bode plots, Nichols charts and the Nyquist criterion, controllability and observability, state feedback, observers, controllers and compensators, and adaptive, nonlinear and noise-driven systems, with appendices of physical models, transform tables, matrix operations and MATLAB.
