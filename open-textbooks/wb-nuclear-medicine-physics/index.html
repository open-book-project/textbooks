    About this book¶

    The physics behind nuclear medicine, written for students of medical imaging: atomic and nuclear structure, radioactive decay, units of radiation, the interaction of radiation with matter, attenuation, gas-filled and scintillation detectors, imaging systems, computers, Fourier methods and image processing, patient dosimetry and the production of radioisotopes, with chapters on dynamic studies and on X-ray CT, ultrasound, MRI and bone densitometry beside nuclear medicine, and multiple-choice questions.
