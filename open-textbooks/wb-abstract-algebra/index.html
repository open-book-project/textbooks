    About this book¶

    An undergraduate course in abstract algebra: the foundations (sets, equivalence relations, functions, the integers), group theory (subgroups, cyclic and permutation groups, homomorphisms, normal subgroups and quotients, group actions, composition series, the Sylow theorems), rings, ideals, integral domains, fraction fields and polynomial rings, fields, vector spaces and modules, algebras (Boolean and Clifford algebras, quaternions), and a glimpse of category theory, lattices and matroids.
