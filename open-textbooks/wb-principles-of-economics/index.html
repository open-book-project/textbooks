    About this book¶

    A short first course in economics with no prerequisites: thinking like an economist, scarcity and opportunity cost, gains from trade, supply and demand, utility and elasticity, budget constraints and indifference curves, labour, wages and the costs of firms, and in macroeconomics GDP, multipliers, money, monetary and fiscal policy, taxation and interest rates, with the Keynesian cross, AS-AD, IS-LM and Phillips curve models.
