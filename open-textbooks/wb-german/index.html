    About this book¶

    A course in German for English speakers: the lessons of each level are set in a German-speaking city and teach conversation, vocabulary and grammar step by step (articles and cases, verbs and tenses, word order), with readings, exercises and review lessons, and appendices on pronunciation and grammar.
