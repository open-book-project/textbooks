    About this book¶

    A two-semester college textbook of biology for science majors: the chemistry of life, the cell, genetics, evolution, the diversity of life, plant and animal structure and function, and ecology. Adapted from Biology 2e by OpenStax (senior contributing authors: Mary Ann Clark, Matthew Douglas, Jung Choi), licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence. Access the original for free at openstax.org. Changes: converted to the web reading format of The Open Book Project: one page per chapter, with the end-of-section summaries, key terms and questions gathered into a review at the end of each chapter, answers folded, mathematics typeset in the page and figures resized for the web. This adaptation is made from the version of the book's source that OpenStax published under CC BY 4.0 (repository openstax/osbooks-biology-bundle, commit d2779c2edf); later versions of the book carry a different licence. The figures keep the credits given in their captions; photographs and illustrations credited to others remain under the terms stated there. The OpenStax name, logo and covers are not used beyond this credit, and OpenStax does not endorse this adaptation.
