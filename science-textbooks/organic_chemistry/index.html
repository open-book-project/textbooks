    About this book¶

    A complete first course in organic chemistry: bonding and shape, naming, stereochemistry, acids and bases, curly-arrow mechanisms, the reactions of hydrocarbons and of the main functional groups, spectroscopy, polymers and biomolecules, and safe, green laboratory practice, with worked examples, exercises and quizzes.
