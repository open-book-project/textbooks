    About this book¶

    A one-semester college textbook of microbiology for students of the health sciences: microscopy, the cell, prokaryotes, eukaryotes, viruses, microbial metabolism, growth and genetics, the control of microbes, antimicrobial drugs, the immune system, and the infectious diseases of each system of the body. Adapted from Microbiology by OpenStax (senior contributing authors: Nina Parker, Mark Schneegurt, Anh-Hue Thi Tu, Philip Lister, Brian M. Forster), licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence. Access the original for free at openstax.org. Changes: converted to the web reading format of The Open Book Project: one page per chapter, with the end-of-section summaries, key terms and questions gathered into a review at the end of each chapter, answers folded, mathematics typeset in the page and figures resized for the web. This adaptation is made from the version of the book's source that OpenStax published under CC BY 4.0 (repository openstax/osbooks-microbiology, commit ecf34dad12); later versions of the book carry a different licence. The figures keep the credits given in their captions; photographs and illustrations credited to others remain under the terms stated there. The OpenStax name, logo and covers are not used beyond this credit, and OpenStax does not endorse this adaptation.
