    Formulas¶

    Formula 1: Factorial¶

    \(n!=n(n-1)(n-2)...(1)\)

    \(0!=1\)

    Formula 2: Combinations¶

    \(\left( \begin{array}{l} n \\ r \end{array} \right)=\frac{n!}{(n-r)!r!}\)

    Formula 3: Binomial Distribution¶

    \(X\;\sim\;B(n,p)\)

    \(P(X=x)=\left( \begin{array}{c} n \\ x \end{array} \right)p^{x}q^{n-x}\), for \(x=0,1,2,...,n\)

    Formula 4: Geometric Distribution¶

    \(X\;\sim\;G(p)\)

    \(P(X=x)=q^{x-1}p\), for \(x=1,2,3,...\)

    Formula 5: Hypergeometric Distribution¶

    \(X\;\sim\;H(r,b,n)\)

    \(P\text{(}X=x\text{)}=\left( \frac{\left( \genfrac{}{}{0pt}{}{r}{x} \right)\left( \genfrac{}{}{0pt}{}{b}{n-x} \right)}{\left( \genfrac{}{}{0pt}{}{r+b}{n} \right)} \right)\)

    Formula 6: Poisson Distribution¶

    \(X\;\sim\;P(\mu)\)

    \(P\text{(}X=x\text{)}=\frac{\mu^{x}e^{-\mu}}{x!}\)

    Formula 7: Uniform Distribution¶

    \(X\;\sim\;U(a,b)\)

    \(f(X)=\frac{1}{b-a}\), \(a \lt x \lt b\)

    Formula 8: Exponential Distribution¶

    \(X\;\sim\;Exp(m)\)

    \(f(x)=me^{-mx}m \gt 0,x\ge 0\)

    Formula 9: Normal Distribution¶

    \(X\;\sim\;N(\mu,\sigma^{2})\)

    \(f\text{(}x\text{)}=\frac{1}{\sigma\sqrt{2\pi}}e^{\frac{{-(x-\mu)}^{2}}{{2\sigma}^{2}}}\) , \(-\infty \lt x \lt \infty\)

    Formula 10: Student's t-distribution¶

    \(X\;\sim\;t_{df}\)

    \(f\text{(}x\text{)}=\frac{{\left( 1+\frac{x^{2}}{n} \right)}^{\frac{-(n+1)}{2}}\Gamma\left( \frac{n+1}{2} \right)}{\sqrt{n\pi}\Gamma\left( \frac{n}{2} \right)}\)

    \(X=\frac{Z}{\sqrt{\frac{Y}{n}}}\)

    \(Z\;\sim\;N(0,1),\;Y\;\sim\;X_{df}^{2}\), \(n\) = degrees of freedom

    Formula 11: Chi-Square Distribution¶

    \(X\;\sim\;X_{df}^{2}\)

    \(f\text{(}x\text{)}=\frac{x^{\frac{n-2}{2}}e^{\frac{-x}{2}}}{2^{\frac{n}{2}}\Gamma\left( \frac{n}{2} \right)}\), \(x \gt 0\) , \(n\) = positive integer and degrees of freedom

    Formula 12: F Distribution¶

    \(X\;\sim\;F_{df(n),df(d)}\)

    \(df(n)\;=\)degrees of freedom for the numerator

    \(df(d)\;=\)degrees of freedom for the denominator

    \(f(x)=\frac{\Gamma(\frac{u+v}{2})}{\Gamma(\frac{u}{2})\Gamma(\frac{v}{2})}{(\frac{u}{v})}^{\frac{u}{2}}x^{(\frac{u}{2}-1)}[1+(\frac{u}{v})x^{-0.5(u+v)}]\)

    \(X=\frac{Y_{u}}{W_{v}}\), \(Y\), \(W\) are chi-square
