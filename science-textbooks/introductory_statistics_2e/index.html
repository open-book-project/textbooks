    About this book¶

    A one-semester introduction to statistics: sampling and data, descriptive statistics, probability, discrete and continuous random variables, the normal distribution, the central limit theorem, confidence intervals, hypothesis testing, the chi-square and F distributions, regression and correlation. Adapted from Introductory Statistics 2e by OpenStax (senior contributing authors: Barbara Illowsky, Susan Dean), licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence. Access the original for free at openstax.org. Changes: converted to the web reading format of The Open Book Project: one page per chapter, with the end-of-section summaries, key terms and questions gathered into a review at the end of each chapter, answers folded, mathematics typeset in the page and figures resized for the web. This adaptation is made from the version of the book's source that OpenStax published under CC BY 4.0 (repository openstax/osbooks-introductory-statistics-bundle, commit 50ec917490); later versions of the book carry a different licence. The figures keep the credits given in their captions; photographs and illustrations credited to others remain under the terms stated there. The OpenStax name, logo and covers are not used beyond this credit, and OpenStax does not endorse this adaptation.
