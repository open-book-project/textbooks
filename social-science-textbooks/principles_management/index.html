    About this book¶

    An introductory textbook of management: managers and their functions, the history of management thought, the environment and ethics of organizations, strategy, decision making, organizational structure and culture, human resources, diversity, leadership, motivation, teams, communication and control. Adapted from Principles of Management by OpenStax (senior contributing authors: David S. Bright, Anastasia H. Cortes and contributing authors), licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence. Access the original for free at openstax.org. Changes: converted to the web reading format of The Open Book Project: one page per chapter, with the end-of-section summaries, key terms and questions gathered into a review at the end of each chapter, answers folded, mathematics typeset in the page and figures resized for the web. This adaptation is made from the version of the book's source that OpenStax published under CC BY 4.0 (repository openstax/osbooks-principles-of-management-bundle, commit a995241fbc); later versions of the book carry a different licence. The figures keep the credits given in their captions; photographs and illustrations credited to others remain under the terms stated there. The OpenStax name, logo and covers are not used beyond this credit, and OpenStax does not endorse this adaptation.
