    About this book¶

    The history of the world from the first humans to 1500: the early civilizations of Mesopotamia, Egypt, India, China and the Americas, the classical empires of Persia, Greece, Rome, India and China, the rise of Christianity and Islam, Africa, the Silk Roads, the Mongols and the world on the eve of European expansion. Adapted from World History, Volume 1, to 1500 by OpenStax (senior contributing authors: Ann Kordas, Ryan J. Lynch, Brooke Nelson, Julie Tatlock), licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence. Access the original for free at openstax.org. Changes: converted to the web reading format of The Open Book Project: one page per chapter, with the end-of-section summaries, key terms and questions gathered into a review at the end of each chapter, answers folded, mathematics typeset in the page and figures resized for the web. This adaptation is made from the version of the book's source that OpenStax published under CC BY 4.0 (repository openstax/osbooks-world-history, commit 3e17beda91); later versions of the book carry a different licence. The figures keep the credits given in their captions; photographs and illustrations credited to others remain under the terms stated there. The OpenStax name, logo and covers are not used beyond this credit, and OpenStax does not endorse this adaptation.
